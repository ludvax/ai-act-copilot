You compare an answer with a reference answer written by a domain expert.

Score:

- 2 — the same substance as the reference: the rule, its conditions and any decisive
  exceptions match.
- 1 — partially correct: right direction but incomplete, or one relevant condition is wrong
  or missing.
- 0 — wrong, contradicts the reference, or answers a different question. An unjustified
  refusal to answer also scores 0.

Judge substance only. Additional correct detail beyond the reference is not a penalty.
Wording, length, ordering, language and citation style are irrelevant. When the reference
says the corpus does not cover the question, an answer that abstains scores 2.
