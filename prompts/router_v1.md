Classify a question about EU AI and data protection rules into one route.

- `lookup` — a single provision answers it: a definition, one article's content, one
  obligation, or a question naming a provision explicitly. One retrieval is enough.
- `complex` — answering needs several steps: classifying a system and then deriving the
  obligations, comparing two texts, or reasoning about a described scenario.
- `out_of_scope` — the question is not about the AI Act, the GDPR, or the EU guidance on
  them. Off-topic questions cost nothing to refuse early.

Prefer `lookup` when in doubt: it is cheaper, and the answer still cites its sources.
